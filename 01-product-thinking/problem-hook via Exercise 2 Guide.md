# Problem Hook & Value Proposition (Module 1)

## Responses
- **Chosen path, which scenario are you committing to? (StreamLine or RouteLogic, or your own):** My own initiative: a scalable, configurable installment-plan framework for BFS.
- **Strategic crisis, based on the brief, if the company does nothing for 12 months, what happens to its market position?:** If we do nothing for 12 months, BFS risks being outscaled by competitors that can expand into new verticals faster, while we remain tied up in customer-specific project work. This increases fragmentation and makes onboarding, market expansion, and regulatory adaptation slower and more expensive.
- **Moment of misery, what is the user likely doing outside the product (Google, spreadsheets, a competitor) because your tool isn’t serving them?:** Clients and internal teams are currently forced to manage similar installment-plan requirements through repeated coordination, bespoke configurations, and individual implementation projects. They often have to manually explain available payment options, answer recurring questions, and manage customer expectations because the product setup is not sufficiently standardized or self-explanatory. Instead of selecting from reusable building blocks, each new client or use case can trigger another round of alignment, configuration, and technical adaptation.
- **Problem hook, summarize the business risk and user pain into one urgent sentence.:** We must prevent increasing product fragmentation from limiting the scalability of BFS's installment-plan business by replacing repeated client-specific configurations with a standardized, configurable installment-plan framework that dynamically applies the right rules for each customer, transaction, client, and market.
- **Value proposition, based on the proposed initiative (Spotlight or Velocity), what is the new value and why is it urgent to launch now?:** For BFS clients that need flexible installment solutions across different customer groups, markets, and use cases, we will provide a standardized but configurable installment-plan framework that applies reusable rules while allowing controlled variation where needed. This creates faster onboarding, easier adaptation to market and regulatory requirements, and less dependency on bespoke implementations. It is urgent because the current project-based approach limits BFS’s ability to scale efficiently into new verticals while complexity continues to grow.
