Installment Plan Self-Service

Give responsible payers clear, safe ways to manage an existing agreement—reducing avoidable support contacts without compromising payment reliability.

Primary metricReduce manual contact rate by 10–15% relative
Delivery team1 PM · 1 Designer · 3 Engineers · 1 QA
GuardrailPayment reliability must not worsen

Delivery roadmap

NOW

6–8 week pilot
3

Removes a direct, rule-based reason to contact support by letting eligible payers change their collection date themselves.

The underlying change already exists in the core system, so securely exposing it in the portal directly replaces a manual support request without changing complex payment or booking logic.

Enables BFS to prove whether the pilot reduces manual contacts and identifies where customers still abandon or escalate.

NEXT

Following 1–2 sprints
3

Reduces avoidable “what happens next?” follow-up contacts when a request cannot be completed automatically.

A narrowly scoped eligible extension/shortening flow directly replaces a key manual request while using the existing configurable framework.

Provides the rules and guardrails needed to safely expose permitted plan changes across agreements, partners, and markets.

LATER

Validated backlog
3

Directly addresses payment-pressure contacts but requires robust schedule, eligibility, and payment-reliability controls.

Can remove urgent failed-payment contacts, but requires careful repayment or re-collection handling without increasing payment risk.

Replaces manual payoff requests but needs binding payoff calculations, payment execution, and reliable account closure.
